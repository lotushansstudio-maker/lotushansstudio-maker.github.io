FIG 03SYS.ABOUT

I like small, honest studies. Pick one question, use only data that was known at the time, and write down where it breaks. The lotus up top is the same idea: noisy observations, a Kalman filter, and a flower that only shows up once the noise is filtered out.我喜欢小而诚实的研究。只问一个问题，只用当时就能拿到的数据，再把它在哪里失效写下来。上面的莲花也是这个意思：带噪声的观测，一个卡尔曼滤波，噪声滤掉之后花才出现。
